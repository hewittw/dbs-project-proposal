I'm choosing between two project ideas and I want feedback on both before I commit.

One line each · the problem and the user

A · Claude Setup Builder: rebuilding your whole Claude setup by hand for every new class, lab or job is slow and intimidating, for anyone who uses Claude in more than one place, from power users to beginners.

B · Personal Tax Calendar: every business has its own tax deadlines and there's no centralized, cheap, intuitive way to see yours, for businesses in general, especially smaller ones.

Why these two ideas

Both came out of my own life. I recently had to set Claude up for my lab and for this class, and moving skills and MCPs between them was harder than it should be. The tax calendar comes from my mom's world as an accountant. I talked the setup idea through with my family (Mom, Dad and my sister), and the tax idea with my mom and two of her accountant colleagues. Those conversations shaped where each idea is now.
